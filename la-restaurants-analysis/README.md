# 🍽️ Los Angeles Restaurant Market

Exploratory analysis of the Los Angeles food market with a focus on decision-making for investors. The project investigates market composition, the profile of chains versus independents, establishment capacity, and geographical distribution by street.

|------|---------|
| **Dataset** | `rest_data_us_upd.csv` |
| **Total records** | 9,648 establishments |
| **City analysed** | Los Angeles, USA |
| **Notebook** | `LA-restaurants.ipynb` |
| **Generated output** | `LA_restaurants_apresentacao.pdf` |

## 📁 About the Dataset

The file `rest_data_us_upd.csv` contains data on food establishments in Los Angeles with the following columns:

| Column | Type | Description |
|--------|------|-----------|
| `id` | int | Unique identifier of the establishment |
| `object_name` | str | Establishment name |
| `address` | str | Full address |
| `chain` | bool | `True` = chain / `False` = independent |
| `object_type` | str | Type: Restaurant, Fast Food, Cafe, Bakery, Bar, Pizza |
| `number` | int | Seating capacity |

---

## 🔍 Analyses Performed

### 1. Proportion by establishment type
Percentage distribution of the 6 establishment types (Restaurant, Fast Food, Café, Bakery, Bar, Pizza) with a horizontal bar chart.

### 2. Chains vs. Independents
- **61.9%** of establishments are independent
- **38.1%** are chains
- Visualisation using a pie chart with direct comparison between the two groups.

### 3. Establishment type by business model
Grouped bars comparing the proportion of each type within the chains group and within the independents group — revealing that **fast food dominates chains** and **traditional restaurants dominate independents**.

### 4. Seating capacity: chains vs. independents
Boxplot comparing the distribution of seats between the two groups, investigating whether chains operate with more units and fewer seats (fast food model) or the opposite.

### 5. Geographical concentration by street
- Top 10 streets with the most establishments in LA
- **Wilshire Blvd** leads with 354 restaurants
- **63.5% of streets** have only 1 restaurant — indicating low saturation outside the main arteries

### 6. Final dashboard (PDF)
Executive presentation consolidating the main KPIs and charts on a single page, exported as a high-quality PDF.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- Traditional restaurants are the dominant format in Los Angeles, especially among independents.
- Fast food is the most representative model for chains — consistent with the profile of franchises and standardised operations.
- The market has a strong presence of local businesses (independents), suggesting room for new entrants with differentiation.
- **Recommendation for investors:** A café or bakery with 30–50 seats on a secondary street represents a local reference opportunity before scaling to a chain, given the low level of competition outside the main avenues.

## 🔍 Análises realizadas

### 1. Proporção por tipo de estabelecimento
Distribuição percentual dos 6 tipos de estabelecimento (Restaurante, Fast Food, Café, Padaria, Bar, Pizza) com gráfico de barras horizontal.

### 2. Redes vs. Independentes
- **61,9%** dos estabelecimentos são independentes
- **38,1%** são redes
- Visualização em gráfico de pizza com comparação direta entre os grupos.

### 3. Tipo de estabelecimento por modelo de negócio
Barras agrupadas comparando a proporção de cada tipo dentro do grupo de redes e dentro do grupo de independentes — revelando que **fast food domina as redes** e **restaurantes tradicionais dominam os independentes**.

### 4. Capacidade de assentos: redes vs. independentes
Boxplot comparando a distribuição de assentos entre os dois grupos, investigando se redes operam com mais unidades e menos assentos (modelo fast food) ou o contrário.

### 5. Concentração geográfica por rua
- Top 10 ruas com mais estabelecimentos em LA

### 6. Dashboard final (PDF)
Apresentação executiva consolidando os principais KPIs e gráficos em uma única página, exportada em PDF de alta qualidade.

## 💡 Principais conclusões

- Restaurantes tradicionais são o formato dominante em Los Angeles, especialmente entre independentes.
- Fast food é o modelo mais representativo das redes — compatível com o perfil de franquias e operações padronizadas.
- O mercado tem forte presença de negócios locais (independentes), sugerindo espaço para novos entrantes com diferenciação.
- **Recomendação para investidores:** Café ou Padaria com 30–50 assentos em rua secundária representa oportunidade de referência local antes de escalar para rede, dado o baixo nível de competição fora das principais avenidas.
