# 📊 Business Analysis

**User Behaviour Analysis, LTV, CAC and ROI by Marketing Channel**

***

## 📋 About the Project

This project was developed as part of the TripleTen Data Analytics Bootcamp. The objective is to carry out a complete business analysis of Y.Afisha — a ticketing and events platform — covering everything from user behaviour to the profitability of marketing channels.

The analysis is based on real data on visits, orders, and marketing costs between **June 2017 and May 2018**, and culminates in well-founded strategic recommendations on budget allocation, customer retention, and channel optimisation.

***

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand user visit and session behaviour over time
- Analyse conversion patterns and the average time between first visit and first purchase
- Calculate LTV (Lifetime Value) by cohort and monitor its growth over time
- Calculate CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) by marketing channel
- Calculate ROMI (Return on Marketing Investment) by cohort and channel
- Compare performance by device (desktop vs. mobile)
- Identify underinvested channels and loss-making channels
- Develop strategic recommendations for the business

***

## 🗂️ Project Structure

### **Stage 1: Dataset Exploration and Optimisation**
- Loading of the three datasets and initial inspection
- Conversion of date columns from `object` to `datetime`
- Validation of data types and consistency

### **Stage 2: User and Session Analysis**
- Count of daily, weekly, and monthly unique users
- Analysis of session duration and user return patterns
- Identification of growth trend (Jun–Nov/2017) and decline (Nov/2017–May/2018)

### **Stage 3: Sales and Conversion Analysis**
- Conversion time: interval between first visit and first purchase
- Distribution of orders per user and identification of one-time buyers
- Calculation of static and dynamic LTV by cohort (based on mean and median)
- Visualisation of LTV evolution over 11 months

### **Stage 4: Costs and ROI Analysis**
- Total expenditure by channel and over time
- Calculation of CAC by marketing channel
- Calculation of ROI by channel (revenue vs. cost)
- Calculation of ROMI by cohort with detailed heatmap
- Conversion analysis by device type (desktop vs. touch)

### **Stage 5: Conclusions and Recommendations**
- Summary of findings by area of analysis
- Data-driven strategic recommendations

***

## 📊 Datasets

### **Files**

| File | Description |
|------|-----------|
| `visits_log_us.csv` | Log of user sessions during the analysed period |
| `orders_log_us.csv` | Log of orders/transactions completed |
| `costs_us.csv` | Marketing expenditure by channel and by day |

### **Column Descriptions**

**visits_log_us.csv**

| Column | Description |
|--------|-----------|
| `Uid` | Unique user identifier |
| `Device` | Device type (`desktop` or `touch`) |
| `Start Ts` | Session start timestamp |
| `End Ts` | Session end timestamp |
| `Source Id` | Acquisition channel that originated the visit |

**orders_log_us.csv**

| Column | Description |
|--------|-----------|
| `Uid` | Unique user identifier |
| `Buy Ts` | Purchase timestamp |
| `Revenue` | Revenue generated by the order ($) |

**costs_us.csv**

| Column | Description |
|--------|-----------|
| `source_id` | Marketing channel identifier |
| `dt` | Expenditure date |
| `costs` | Amount invested in the channel on that day ($) |

## 📈 Key Results

### **User Behaviour**

| Metric | Result |
|---------|-----------|
| Peak monthly users | 32,797 (November/2017) |
| Decline to end of period | 20,701 (May/2018) |
| Average return interval | ~28 days |
| Average conversion time | 16.9 days after first visit |
| Median conversion time | ~16 minutes (50% convert very quickly) |

### **Sales and LTV**

| Metric | Result |
|---------|-----------|
| Converted users | 36,523 |
| One-time buyers | 30,231 (82.8%) |
| Average order value | ~$5.00 |
| LTV in month 0 | $4.80 |
| LTV in month 11 | $11.88 (+147%) |
| Break-even (ROMI = 1×) | Month 8 after acquisition |

### **Costs and ROI by Channel**

| Channel | Investment | ROI | Assessment |
|-------|:------------:|:---:|-----------|
| Source 1 | Low | **+387.83%** | ✅ Underinvested — top priority |
| Source 9 | Low | **+240.91%** | ✅ Excellent return — scale up |
| Source 2 | Medium | **+165.21%** | ✅ Solid — consider expansion |
| Source 5 | Medium | **+115.49%** | ✅ Profitable — consider expansion |
| Source 4 | High | +96.32% | ⚠️ Below expectations |
| Source 10 | Medium | +41.15% | ⚠️ Poor performance |
| Source 3 | **43% of budget** | **-15.66%** | ❌ Loss — redistribute budget |

### **Performance by Device**

| Device | Unique users | Conversion rate |
|-------------|:---------------:|:-----------------:|
| Desktop | 70% of total | 17.76% |
| Mobile (touch) | 30% of total | 10.23% |

> Desktop converts **73% more** than mobile — there is a clear opportunity for mobile device optimisation.

# 📊 Análise de Negócio

**Análise de Comportamento de Usuários, LTV, CAC e ROI por Canal de Marketing**

***

## 📋 Sobre o Projeto

Este projeto foi desenvolvido como parte do bootcamp TripleTen de Data Analytics. O objetivo é realizar uma análise completa do negócio da Y.Afisha — plataforma de ingressos e eventos — cobrindo desde o comportamento dos usuários até a rentabilidade dos canais de marketing.

A análise parte de dados reais de visitas, pedidos e custos de marketing entre **junho de 2017 e maio de 2018**, e culmina em recomendações estratégicas fundamentadas sobre alocação de orçamento, retenção de clientes e otimização de canais.

***

## 🎯 Objetivos

- Entender o comportamento de visitas e sessões dos usuários ao longo do tempo
- Analisar padrões de conversão e o tempo médio entre primeira visita e primeira compra
- Calcular LTV (Lifetime Value) por coorte e monitorar seu crescimento ao longo do tempo
- Calcular CAC (Custo de Aquisição por Cliente) por canal de marketing
- Calcular ROMI (Return on Marketing Investment) por coorte e canal
- Comparar performance por dispositivo (desktop vs. mobile)
- Identificar canais subinvestidos e canais com prejuízo
- Elaborar recomendações estratégicas para o negócio

***

## 🗂️ Estrutura do Projeto

### **Etapa 1: Exploração e Otimização dos Datasets**
- Carregamento dos três datasets e inspeção inicial
- Conversão de colunas de data de `object` para `datetime`
- Validação de tipos e consistência dos dados

### **Etapa 2: Análise de Usuários e Sessões**
- Contagem de usuários únicos diários, semanais e mensais
- Análise da duração das sessões e padrão de retorno dos usuários
- Identificação de tendência de crescimento (jun–nov/2017) e declínio (nov/2017–mai/2018)

### **Etapa 3: Análise de Vendas e Conversão**
- Tempo de conversão: intervalo entre primeira visita e primeira compra
- Distribuição de pedidos por usuário e identificação de one-time buyers
- Cálculo de LTV estático e dinâmico por coorte (baseado em média e mediana)
- Visualização da evolução do LTV ao longo de 11 meses

### **Etapa 4: Análise de Custos e ROI**
- Total de gastos por canal e ao longo do tempo
- Cálculo do CAC por canal de marketing
- Cálculo do ROI por canal (receita vs. custo)
- Cálculo do ROMI por coorte com heatmap detalhado
- Análise de conversão por tipo de dispositivo (desktop vs. touch)

### **Etapa 5: Conclusões e Recomendações**
- Síntese dos achados por área de análise
- Recomendações estratégicas baseadas nos dados

***
